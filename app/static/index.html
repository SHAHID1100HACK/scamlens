How it works

Four independent checks feed one score: rule-based evidence (fees, OTP requests, lookalike or brand-new domains), a trained text classifier, retrieval over a library of scam playbooks, and an AI model that reasons over all of it. Every highlighted warning is a verbatim quote from your text, and every playbook shown was actually retrieved. If the AI is unavailable ScamLens switches to limited mode and says so.

Security & privacy

Nothing you submit is stored. Card numbers and OTP-like values are masked before analysis. Inputs are treated as untrusted: instructions hidden in your text cannot change the verdict, results render as plain text only, and requests are rate-limited. ScamLens can be wrong; always verify through official channels.
